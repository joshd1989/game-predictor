    Daily parlays

    Small keeps the 2 or 3 strongest model sides (3 only if they still combine to 35% or better) and needs 3 priced games. Big keeps 4 to 6 sides, as many as stay at 15% or better, and needs 5 priced games. Wild is a long shot aimed at +1000 or longer: 5 to 8 sides chosen to maximize the model's expected value, preferring a side where the model beats the no-vig price when two tickets tie. One leg per game, best posted price, real moneylines only. A day is left out of a ticket when it does not have enough priced games, and Wild is left out when no 5–8 leg ticket reaches +1000.

    Parlay odds multiply the book's margin on every leg, so the house edge grows as you add legs. Legs are treated as independent. The model has lost money against closing lines in its NHL and NBA backtests, so treat every ticket, and especially Wild, as a long shot rather than a lock. The model probability shown is the model's real combined chance.

    Best value picks

    Edge is the model win probability minus the market's no-vig win probability. EV and the stake use the best price found across the books. A gap above 12 points often means the market has lineup news the model has not priced.
